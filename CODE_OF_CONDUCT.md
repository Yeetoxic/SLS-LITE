# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SLS-LITE welcomes constructive participation regardless of experience,
identity, background, or role. Be respectful, assume good faith while evidence
is incomplete, criticize ideas rather than people, and make technical feedback
specific enough to act on.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing
another person's private information, sustained disruption, or encouraging
others to engage in that behavior.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release coordination, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is
publicly representing SLS-LITE.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Do not start a public confrontation or expose private evidence. Report conduct
concerns privately through the repository's **Security** tab and prefix the
report title with `Conduct report`. That private route is also available for
conduct reports even when the concern is not a software vulnerability.

Include links or screenshots, the relevant dates, and a concise description of
the impact. Remove unrelated personal information. Maintainers will review the
report as privately as practical, hear affected parties when appropriate, and
take proportionate action. Responses may include a private warning, removal of
content, temporary participation restrictions, or a permanent ban.

Retaliation against a good-faith reporter or participant in a review is itself
a violation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may also result in action.
